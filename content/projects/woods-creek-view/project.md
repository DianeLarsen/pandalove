## The problem

Neighborhood information is usually scattered across Facebook groups, email chains, hand-lettered signs, and the ancient rite of hoping someone remembers to tell you. Woods Creek View creates one focused, community-owned place for the information residents actually need.

## What I am building

Woods Creek View is a local community website with announcements, event listings, and resident-submitted updates. It is meant to be useful to people who live nearby, rather than a generic social network attempting to manufacture a community from engagement metrics.

## Product focus

- Clear, easy-to-find neighborhood announcements.
- Event listings that make local activities visible.
- A submission path for residents to contribute updates.
- A responsive, simple interface that works for different comfort levels with technology.

## Engineering focus

This project is a practical web-product exercise: turning a local need into a clear information architecture, designing the content flows, and creating a simple interface that does not require users to learn a new hobby just to find an event.

## Next steps

Resume active development, define the current submission and moderation workflow, update the visual system, and prioritize the features that make the site worth returning to for neighbors.
